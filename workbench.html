The survey that plants a seed.

A proposed ten-question template based on the method. Adapt the wording to your organization, and tell people how their answers will be used before you collect them.

  1. Have you used an AI tool for work? If yes, how often?
  2. Would you like to use AI in your work? Why or why not?
  3. Which AI tools have you tried, at work or elsewhere?
  4. What part of your work has AI helped with?
  5. Where has AI disappointed you, failed, or made more work?
  6. Which recurring task takes more time than it should?
  7. What concerns would you want addressed before using AI here?
  8. What access, permissions, or support would help you begin?
  9. What would make a first AI session genuinely useful to you?
  10. If this AI stuff turns out to be the crazy cool thing that actually does make work better, what is that moonshot project?

Write it like a short story.

Start on paper if you can. When you are ready, use this space to turn the story into a working brief. Keep confidential information out of practice exercises.

Who is doing the work? What is difficult? What do they have to work with?
Name the steps, the decisions, the inputs, and the places a human must check.
Describe an observable result, not just “better” or “faster.”
Would someone who does not know the story get to the same place?
