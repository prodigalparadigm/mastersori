# Mastersori: the day-one survey

A proposed working template by Kathleen Bartin, Prodigal Paradigm LLC.
First edition · November 2026

## Before you send it

Use an organization-approved survey channel. Explain the purpose, whether responses are identifiable, who may see them, and how long they will be retained. Do not collect confidential examples or employee-sensitive information unnecessarily.

## Ten questions

1. Have you used an AI tool for work? If yes, how often?

2. Would you like to use AI in your work? Why or why not?

3. Which AI tools have you tried, at work or elsewhere?

4. What part of your work has AI helped with?

5. Where has AI disappointed you, failed, or made more work?

6. Which recurring task would you change? About how long does one instance take now, including checking, and how often do you do it? An estimate or 'not sure' is fine.

7. What concerns would you want addressed before using AI here?

8. What access, permissions, or support would help you begin?

9. What would make a first AI session genuinely useful to you?

10. If this AI stuff turns out to be the crazy cool thing that actually does make work better, what is that moonshot project?

## The facilitator's note

The first nine questions take the temperature. The tenth plants a seed. Return to the moonshot after the first hands-on session. Treat pro, resistant, and already-enabled as working observations, not permanent labels for people. Meet department heads separately and compare the two maps.

## Status

This is a starter template, not a validated assessment or the final wording of a previously administered survey.
